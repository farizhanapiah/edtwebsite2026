Media Asset Plan
Designer Task List — 2026

Prepared: March 2026  ·  20 Projects  ·  Confidential

Context

The EDT website currently has no project media assets. All 20 project cards on /work render as empty dark-blue placeholder boxes, and all 6 full case study pages contain zero images or videos. This document is the complete asset checklist for the designer to produce visuals that will be integrated directly into the live website.

Recommended Dimensions & Formats

Asset TypeDimensionsFormatNotes
Thumbnail (work listing card)1600 × 1000 pxJPG, quality 8516:10 ratio; displayed at ~640×400 on desktop
Hero / Banner (case study top)1920 × 1080 pxJPG, quality 85Full-width cinematic; cropped to 16:9
Gallery — Landscape1600 × 900 pxJPG, quality 85Used in image grids and feature sections
Gallery — Portrait900 × 1200 pxJPG, quality 85Use sparingly; good for in-space people shots
Gallery — Square1200 × 1200 pxJPG, quality 85Good for UI screenshots, app close-ups
Video — Hero / Embed1920 × 1080 pxMP4 (H.264)16:9; keep under 30 MB for web
Video — Poster / Thumbnail1920 × 1080 pxJPGStatic first-frame shown before video plays
OG Image (social sharing)1200 × 630 pxJPGOne per project; text-safe zone: centre 60%
Export all JPGs at sRGB colour space, progressive encoding. Filenames must be lowercase with hyphens — no spaces, no capitals.

Naming Convention

/public/images/[project-slug]/[project-slug]_[type]_[##].jpg
/public/videos/[project-slug]/[project-slug]_[type]_[##].mp4
TokenValuesExample
[project-slug]Exact slug from tables belowmetahrise
[type]thumb, hero, gallery, og, video, postergallery
[##]Two-digit number from 01; omit for single assets (thumb, hero, og)03
[ext]jpg, mp4jpg

metahrise_hero.jpg
